# Supply Chain Performance & Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive Power BI project designed to monitor supply chain performance and support data-driven operational decisions.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Supply Chain Overview** — Sales, profit, inventory, and delivery KPIs.
2. **Procurement & Supplier Analytics** — Purchase spending, supplier quality, and delivery performance.
3. **Inventory Performance & Optimization** — Stock levels, inventory value, stockouts, and replenishment.
4. **Logistics & Delivery Performance** — Shipment status, freight costs, and on-time delivery.
5. **Demand & Forecasting Analytics** — Actual versus forecast demand and variance analysis.
6. **Supply Chain Control Tower** — Consolidated operational KPIs and risk monitoring.

## Tools & Skills
